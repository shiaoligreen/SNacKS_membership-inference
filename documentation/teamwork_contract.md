The goal of this agreement is to support fair collaboration, clear communication, and shared accountability throughout the duration of the project.

We view this agreement as a guide to help our team work effectively together and create a positive collaboration environment. If needed, the agreement may be revisited and updated with the consent of all team members.

## Work distribution
The team agrees that work will be distributed in a fairly, taking into consideration each member’s strengths, learning objectives, availability, and current workload. Our objective is to distribute tasks as evenly as possible in terms  time commitment, task complexity and personal preferences.
Tasks will typically be assigned during the first part of the week (Monday or Tuesday). Task assignment will be managed through the project’s GitHub repository, primarily by creating issues and assigning collaborators to them.
If at any point a team member feels that task distribution is unclear or uneven, they agree to raise the concern early so that the team can adjust responsibilities.

## Expected Work Hours and Code Contributions
Each team member agrees to dedicate a minimum of 5 hours per week to the project. 
Work contributions are expected to be continuous and transparent, with code and other materials uploaded according to the team’s agreed schedule. 
Team members commit to uploading code regularly and major contributions should be uploaded by Friday to allow sufficient time for review and integration.

## Group Meetings
The team will hold regular meetings in order to coordinate progress, solve challenges, and  align project goals. 
Meetings will occur once a week, typically on Monday or Tuesday after class. Team members are expected to attend scheduled meetings whenever possible, and to notify the group in advance if they cannot attend. These meetings are flexible and can be rescheduled according to availability. 
To reduce the need for additional meetings outside of class time, the team also agrees to use Thursday lab sessions (2:00 PM for Block 5 and TBD for Block 6) as collaborative working time when possible.

## Working Style
The team agrees to adopt the following working style: At the beginning of each week, team members will review the project deliverables and familiarize themselves with the goals for that sprint or milestone.
Mondays and Tuesdays will generally be used for organizing tasks and assigning responsibilities so that each member can plan their work accordingly.
If needed, in-person discussions can take place after Tuesday’s class.
Once tasks are assigned, team members will work independently on their responsibilities during the week. On Thursdays, the team will use lab time to continue working collaboratively, discuss progress, and address any challenges that arise.

## Code Review
Code review is an important part of our workflow and helps maintain code quality while allowing team members to learn from each other’s work.
All significant code contributions should be submitted through the Git repository using pull requests. At least one other team member should review the contribution before it is merged into the main branch. Reviewers are encouraged to provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement when necessary. The team will split into two sub-teams to have regular pair code reviews. 
The goal of code review is not only to identify potential issues, but also to ensure that all team members understand the evolving codebase. Team members are expected to approach code reviews respectfully and collaboratively, focusing on improving the overall quality and clarity of the project.

## Communication
Effective communication is essential to the success of the team and integer completion of the project. All team members agree to maintain clear, respectful and consistent communication throughout the duration of the project. 
The primary communication channel will be Slack. Team members agree to use this channel to share updates, ask questions, and notify the group of any changes related to tasks or progress.
Each member is responsible for keeping the team informed about their work, including completed tasks, new developments, or potential issues. If tasks need to be split, modified, removed, or added, these changes should be communicated through Slack.
Team members are expected to check the communication channel at least once every 24 hours so that discussions and decisions can move forward efficiently. 

## Documentation
Maintaining clear and organized documentation is an important priority for the team. Project documentation will be maintained in two locations. The main written material will be stored and edited in the team’s Overleaf workspace located at [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/project/69a7482f8fba1434312336cb). Code, technical documentations and any additional project materials will be stored in the team’s Git repository located at [Github](https://github.ubc.ca/shiaolig/SNacKS_membership-inference). 

## Quality standards 
Each team member commits to producing work that meets agreed standards of quality. This includes writing clear, readable, and maintainable code, documenting decisions when necessary, and testing contributions before submission. The team agrees to support a culture of professionalism, responsibility, and continuous learning while working together on this project.

## Availability and scheduling constraints
Team members will communicate any periods of unavailability or scheduling constraints in advance to support effective planning. Known unavailable times include: Thursdays after 7pm and everyday before 9:00am. Members agree to respect one another’s external obligations while ensuring that commitments to the project are fulfilled.

## Conflict Resolution
The team recognizes that disagreements or misunderstandings may occasionally arise during collaborative work. If this happens, team members agree to address concerns openly and respectfully with the goal of finding a constructive solution.
If a conflict arises, the team will first attempt to resolve the issue through discussion between the involved members. If the issue cannot be resolved through direct communication, the team will discuss the matter together during a meeting and work collectively to find a fair solution. If a greater problem comes up, the team can escalate the problem to obtain instructor's input.
All team members agree to approach conflicts with professionalism, patience, and respect for different perspectives, keeping the overall success of the project as the primary priority.
